ICU Liberation

The ICU Liberation Campaign aims to liberate patients from the harmful effects of an intensive care unit stay.

What is the ICU Liberation Campaign?

The ICU Liberation Campaign aims to liberate patients from the harmful effects of an ICU stay and provides evidence-based strategies in a comprehensive bundle for the entire multiprofessional critical care team.
