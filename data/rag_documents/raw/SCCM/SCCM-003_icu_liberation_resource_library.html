ICU Liberation Resources

Explore the ICU Liberation Bundle (A-F), implementation tools and best practices, and more.

The ICU Liberation Campaign provides adult and pediatric critical care teams with a variety of resources to champion and sustain bundle implementation.

Implementation Resources

Implementation Resources

ICU Liberation Bundle (A-F)

The ICU Liberation Bundle is a free collection of resources with strategies on how to apply the PADIS guideline recommendations to every ICU patient.

Learn More

ICU Liberation Implementation Toolkit

Download the free ICU Liberation Implementation Toolkit, and access implementation framework, data metrics, compliance definitions, data collection forms and performance reports.

Access Now

Electronic Health Record Integration

The SCCM ICU Liberation Committee has partnered with Epic and Oracle (formerly Cerner) to incorporate the ICU Liberation Bundle for adult and pediatric patients into clinicians’ workflows.

Bedside Resources

The ICU Liberation Bundle offers several bedside resources for adult and pediatric patients. Find quick access below, or review these in the context of the whole bundle.

Bedside Tools and Calculators for 

Adult Patients

Pain Assessment

  • Numerical Rating Scale (NRS)
  • Behavioral Pain Scale (BPS)
  • Critical-Care Pain Observation Tool (CPOT)
  • Behavioral Pain Scale (BPS) for Pain Assessment in Intubated Patients

Both Spontaneous Awakening Trials (SATs) and 
Spontaneous Breathing Trials (SBTs)

  • Wake Up and Breathe Protocol

Choice of Analgesia and Sedation

  • Richmond-Agitation Sedation Scale (RASS)
  • Riker Sedation-Agitation Scale (SAS)

Delirium Assessment

  • Confusion Assessment Method for the ICU (CAM-ICU)
    • CAM-ICU Training Manual
    • CAM-ICU Flowsheet
    • CAM-ICU Assessment Tool
    • CAM-ICU Bedside Calculator
  • Intensive Care Delirium Screening Checklist (ICDSC)

Bedside Tools and Calculators for 

Pediatric Patients

Pain Assessment

  • Revised Face, Legs, Activity, Cry, Consolability (r-FLACC)
  • FACES pain scale
  • Numerical Rating Scale (NRS)
  • Oucher Scale

Choice of Analgesia and Sedation

  • Richmond-Agitation Sedation Scale (RASS)
  • State Behavioral Scale (SBS)
  • COMFORT-B Scale

Delirium Assessment 

  • Cornell Assessment of Pediatric Delirium (CAPD)
  • Confusion Assessment Method for the ICU (CAM-ICU)
    1. Pediatric CAM-ICU
    2. Preschool CAM-ICU

ICU Liberation

ICU Liberation, Second Edition, eBook

ICU Liberation, Second Edition offers practical application strategies for frontline staff to implement the ICU Liberation Bundle. The book offers strategies for effective interprofessional team-based care in the ICU, performance improvement methods, and the use of data to drive implementation of the bundle. Topics addressed in this new edition include clarifying the role sleep plays in recovery from critical illness, examining updated data collection methods, providing insight into patient and family challenges beyond the ICU, and implementing the ICU Liberation bundle in the pediatric population.
